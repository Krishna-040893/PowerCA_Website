## 4. COMMISSION STRUCTURE

### 4.1 Commission Rate

- **10% commission** on all successful paid referrals
- Commission is calculated on the net subscription value (excluding taxes)
- Commission applies to the initial subscription payment only (not recurring)

### 4.2 Qualifying Referrals

A referral is considered "qualified" when:

- The referred client signs up using your unique referral code
- The client completes payment for a PowerCA subscription
- The payment is successfully processed and not refunded
- The client is a new customer (not an existing PowerCA user)

### 4.3 Cookie Duration

- Referral cookies are valid for 30 days from the initial click
- If a customer makes a purchase within 30 days, you receive credit for the sale

## 5. PAYMENT TERMS

### 5.1 Payment Schedule

- Commissions are paid monthly
- Payments are processed on the 15th of each month for the previous month's earnings
- Minimum payout threshold: ₹1,000 (One Thousand Indian Rupees)
- Earnings below the threshold roll over to the next month

### 5.2 Payment Method

- Payments are made via direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS)
- You must provide valid bank account details
- Bank details must match the name on your affiliate account

### 5.3 Tax Compliance

- You are responsible for all applicable taxes on commission income
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) will be applied as per Indian tax laws
- PowerCA will provide necessary tax documentation (Form 16A, etc.)
- GST-registered affiliates must provide valid GSTIN

### 5.4 Payment Disputes

- Report payment discrepancies within 15 days of payment date
- Disputes reported after 15 days may not be processed
- All payment decisions by PowerCA are final
