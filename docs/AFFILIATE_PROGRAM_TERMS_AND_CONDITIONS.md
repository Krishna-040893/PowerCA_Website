## 1. Program Overview

### What is the PowerCA Affiliate Program?

The PowerCA Affiliate Program is a referral partnership opportunity where individuals and businesses can earn commissions by referring clients to PowerCA - a comprehensive CA practice management SaaS solution.

### Key Benefits

- **10% Commission** on every successful referral
- **Unlimited Earning Potential** - Refer as many customers as you want
- **Real-Time Tracking** - Monitor all your referrals in your affiliate dashboard
- **Professional Support** - Dedicated affiliate support team
- **Marketing Resources** - Access to promotional materials and assets
- **Monthly Payouts** - Regular commission payments

### Who Can Join?

- Chartered Accountants (CAs)
- Tax Consultants
- Business Consultants
- Financial Advisors
- Software Resellers
- Marketing Agencies
- Individual Entrepreneurs
- Companies and Partnerships

## 2. Affiliate Process

### Step 1: Application & Registration

1. **Submit Application**
   - Visit: https://powerca.in/affiliate-program/register
   - Complete the registration form with accurate information
   - Provide business/payment details for commission processing

2. **Required Information**
   - Personal Information (Name, Email, Phone)
   - Business Type (Individual/Company/Partnership)
   - Promotion Strategy (minimum 50 characters)
   - Target Audience Description
   - Bank Account Details (for commission payments)
   - PAN Number (mandatory for tax compliance)
   - GST Number (if applicable)

3. **Application Review**
   - Review Period: 1-2 business days
   - Status Notification: Email notification sent upon approval/rejection
   - Approval Criteria: Quality of promotion strategy, target audience alignment, business credibility

### Step 2: Account Activation

Upon approval, you will receive:

- **Unique Affiliate ID** (e.g., AFF-12345)
- **Unique Referral Code** (e.g., 1EA497C4)
- **Access to Affiliate Dashboard** at https://powerca.in/affiliate/account
- **Welcome Email** with onboarding instructions

### Step 3: Referring Customers

1. **Create Referral**
   - Login to your affiliate dashboard
   - Enter customer details (Name, Email, Phone)
   - System generates unique **Customer ID** (e.g., CUS001, CUS002, etc.)
   - Each customer gets a personalized referral link

2. **Referral Link Format**

   ```
   https://powerca.in/pricing?ref=YOUR_REFERRAL_CODE&cus=CUSTOMER_ID
   ```

3. **Share with Customer**
   - Referral link automatically sent to customer's email
   - Copy link from dashboard to share via WhatsApp, SMS, or other channels
   - Customer clicks link and completes purchase

4. **Multiple Referrals**
   - You can refer **unlimited customers**
   - Each customer gets a unique Customer ID
   - Your referral code remains the same for all customers
   - All referrals tracked separately in your dashboard

### Step 4: Tracking & Monitoring

**Affiliate Dashboard Features:**

- **Referral Summary Cards**
  - Total Referrals Count
  - Paid Customers Count
  - Total Commission Earned
  - Pending Commission Amount

- **Detailed Referral Table**
  - Customer ID and Details
  - Referral Date
  - Payment Status (Pending/Completed)
  - Payment Amount
  - Commission Amount
  - Commission Payment Status (Pending/Paid)

**Real-Time Status Updates:**

- Referral Status: Pending → Completed (when customer pays)
- Commission Status: Pending → Paid (when admin releases payment)

### Step 5: Commission Payment

1. **Customer Completes Payment**
   - System automatically calculates 10% commission
   - Commission shows as "⏳ Pending" in your dashboard
   - Admin receives notification of commission due

2. **Admin Review & Approval**
   - Admin reviews payment and commission eligibility
   - Admin clicks "Mark Paid" button in admin panel
   - Commission status updates to "✓ Paid"

3. **Payment Processing**
   - Commission transferred to your registered bank account
   - Payment frequency: Monthly
   - Payment method: Direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS)
   - Invoice provided for tax records

## 4. Payment Terms

### Payment Schedule

- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Payment Date:** 7th of every month for previous month's earnings
- **Minimum Payout:** ₹1,000 (commissions below this are carried forward)

### Payment Method

- **Primary:** Direct Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS)
- **Required Details:**
  - Bank Account Number
  - IFSC Code
  - Account Holder Name (must match affiliate name)
  - PAN Number (mandatory)

### Payment Processing Time

1. **Commission Earned:** When customer pays
2. **Verification Period:** 5-7 business days (for refund/chargeback checks)
3. **Admin Approval:** 1-2 business days
4. **Payment Transfer:** Next monthly payment cycle
5. **Total Time:** Typically 30-45 days from sale date

### Tax Compliance

- **TDS Deduction:** As per Indian Income Tax Act
- **PAN Mandatory:** Required for all payments
- **GST Invoice:** Affiliates with GST registration must provide tax invoice
- **Form 16A:** TDS certificate issued quarterly

### Payment Statements

Monthly statements include:

- List of all referrals
- Payment amounts
- Commission calculated
- TDS deducted
- Net payout amount
- Transaction reference numbers

   - Customer data must be handled per privacy laws

### 6.2 Commission Rules

1. **Attribution**
   - Commission credited to the affiliate whose link was used
   - Last-click attribution model (most recent referral link wins)
   - Cookie duration: 30 days from click

2. **Self-Referrals**
   - Affiliates cannot earn commission on their own purchases
   - Family member purchases reviewed case-by-case
   - Fraudulent self-referrals result in immediate termination

3. **Duplicate Referrals**
   - If customer already in PowerCA system, no commission
   - Existing customers upgrading: no commission
   - Commission only on new customer acquisitions

4. **Refunds & Chargebacks**
   - Commission reversed if customer receives refund
   - Already paid commissions must be returned
   - Repeated chargebacks may result in account suspension

### 6.3 Payment Guarantees

- PowerCA commits to pay valid commissions within stated timelines
- Delays beyond 60 days entitle affiliate to inquiry and explanation
- Payment disputes must be raised within 30 days of payment date
- PowerCA's records are final for commission calculations

### 6.4 Account Management

1. **Account Security**
   - Affiliates responsible for account security
   - Do not share login credentials
   - Report suspected unauthorized access immediately

2. **Multiple Accounts**
   - One affiliate account per person/business entity
   - Multiple accounts for same entity are prohibited
   - Violation results in suspension of all accounts

3. **Account Updates**
   - Keep contact information current
   - Update bank details with proper verification
   - Notify PowerCA of business structure changes

### 6.5 Modifications

- PowerCA may modify program terms with 30 days notice
- Notice provided via email and affiliate dashboard
- Continued participation after notice constitutes acceptance

## 7. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

### 7.1 Marketing Violations

❌ **Search Engine Manipulation**

- Bidding on PowerCA brand keywords in PPC campaigns
- Domain names containing "PowerCA" or variations
- Creating websites that impersonate PowerCA

❌ **Spam & Unsolicited Marketing**

- Mass unsolicited emails (spam)
- Posting in irrelevant forums or groups
- Automated bot referrals
- Fake reviews or testimonials

❌ **Misleading Claims**

- False price information or fake discounts
- Unauthorized guarantees or warranties
- Misrepresenting features or capabilities
- Using "official," "authorized," or similar terms without permission

### 7.2 Fraudulent Activities

❌ **Commission Fraud**

- Cookie stuffing or forced redirects
- Fake customer information
- Creating fake purchases
- Manipulating referral links

❌ **Identity Fraud**

- Using someone else's identity to register
- Creating referrals for non-existent customers
- Falsifying business credentials

### 7.3 Competitive Activities

❌ **Conflict of Interest**

- Promoting direct competitors on same platform
- Disparaging PowerCA or competitors
- Reverse engineering PowerCA software
- Recruiting PowerCA employees

### 7.4 Penalties for Violations

1. **First Offense:** Warning and 30-day probation
2. **Second Offense:** Suspension for 90 days, pending commissions withheld
3. **Third Offense:** Permanent termination, all commissions forfeited
4. **Serious Fraud:** Immediate termination, legal action, recovery of paid commissions

## 8. Termination Policy

### 8.1 Voluntary Termination (By Affiliate)

- Affiliates may terminate participation at any time
- Submit termination request to affiliates@powerca.in
- Pending commissions (verified sales) will be paid per schedule
- Access to affiliate account removed within 7 days
- No penalties for voluntary termination

### 8.2 Termination by PowerCA

PowerCA may terminate affiliate accounts for:

1. **With Notice (30 Days)**
   - Program restructuring or discontinuation
   - Prolonged inactivity (6+ months without referrals)
   - Business strategy changes

2. **Immediate Termination (No Notice)**
   - Violation of prohibited activities
   - Fraudulent or illegal activities
   - Misrepresentation or material breach
   - Damage to PowerCA reputation
   - Non-compliance with legal requirements

### 8.3 Effects of Termination

Upon termination:

- Affiliate access to dashboard revoked
- Referral links deactivated immediately
- Pending commissions (verified sales) paid per normal schedule
- Unverified commissions subject to review
- Affiliate must cease all promotional activities
- Return of promotional materials if requested
- Confidentiality obligations continue

### 8.4 Appeals Process

- Affiliates may appeal termination within 15 days
- Submit appeal to affiliates@powerca.in with supporting documentation
- PowerCA reviews and responds within 10 business days
- Decision after appeal is final
